Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy applies to the official TV Flick Windows and Android apps and this site, maintained under the GitHub account Gghz007.

Local library

TV Flick stores your library, watch marks, progress, favorites, source settings, and preferences on your device. Windows stores the library in the portable Local Database folder and uses per-user storage for caches, recovery files, and local diagnostics. Android uses Android-managed storage. Local exports go to a destination you choose. TV Flick does not upload its local crash logs or send its library to a TV Flick server. Official builds have no advertising trackers or analytics SDKs.

Google sign-in and backups

Google sign-in is optional. TV Flick requests openid, email, and profile to identify the connected account and display your name, email and photo, when available. It retains the email association in local backup settings. Google handles your password. Windows protects its session with Windows data protection. Android uses Google Play services for authorization and stores the local connection state.

The https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope lets TV Flick create, list, and read its own backup files in its hidden Google Drive app folder. TV Flick cannot read other Drive files through this scope. The app reads backup names, IDs, and dates to show your backup list. Backups may contain library metadata, watch history and progress, favorites, source settings, and optional artwork. OAuth tokens, API credentials, and resolved video stream URLs are excluded.

Exports and restores travel between your device and Google Drive over HTTPS. Backups run when you manually export. If you enable AutoExport, TV Flick checks whether a backup is due on app startup.

Google user data and other providers

TV Flick handles information received from Google APIs in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google account data is used for the connected profile and backup features. TV Flick does not sell it, use it for advertising or AI training, or send it to video sources or metadata providers.

TMDB receives searches and requests for metadata and artwork. External video sources, YouTube, and your selected download app may receive the requests or media information needed for the action you choose. Configured builds can request release information from GitHub or fetch signed filtering rules. Providers can see standard network information such as your IP address. GitHub Pages can log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you reset or remove it. Use Settings → Profile → Reset Library for the selected library data. Clear Android app storage or remove the Windows local database and per-user app data while TV Flick is closed for a broader reset. Delete local exports separately.

Signing out removes the local sign-in session and disables AutoExport. The stored email association can remain until local settings are cleared. You can revoke authorization in your Google Account connections. To remove cloud backups, open Google Drive on a computer and look for the delete hidden app data option in Settings → Manage apps → TV Flick. About the app-data folder.

Contact and updates

Use the support email shown on TV Flick’s Google consent screen for private requests or the public issue tracker for general questions. This page carries the current revision date. We will update the disclosure and request new consent if required when Google data use changes materially.
